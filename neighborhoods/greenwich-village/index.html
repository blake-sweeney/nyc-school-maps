Public elementary, middle and high schools for families in Greenwich Village: zoned schools, schools without a zone and high schools, with ratings and test scores.

The neighborhood

Greenwich Village is in Manhattan, in school District 2. 3 elementary school zones cover it. Zone lines don’t follow neighborhood lines, so the zoned school can change from one block to the next.

Always confirm your exact address on schoolsearch.schools.nyc or by searching it on the map.
