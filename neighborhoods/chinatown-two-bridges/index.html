Chinatown-Two Bridges school zones

Public elementary, middle and high schools for families in Chinatown-Two Bridges: zoned schools, schools without a zone and high schools, with ratings and test scores.

The neighborhood

Chinatown-Two Bridges is in Manhattan, in school District 2 and District 1. Different parts of Chinatown-Two Bridges are zoned for 5 elementary schools. Zone lines don’t follow neighborhood lines, so the zoned school can change from one block to the next.

Elementary schools

Open the elementary school map →

District 1 has no elementary school zones: elementary schools there give priority to families who live in the district. Chinatown-Two Bridges is split between District 2 (about 80%) and District 1 (about 20%). Your district depends on your exact address, like your zone.

Before you start

  • This is an independent map made by a NYC parent. It isn’t made by, affiliated with or endorsed by NYC Public Schools.
  • Zone lines come from the DOE’s Find a School map (Oct 2026). Ratings, test scores and programs come from the DOE’s 2024–25 School Quality Snapshot. Data can be out of date or have errors.
  • Zones change from year to year, and blocks near a boundary are easy to misread on a map.
  • Living in a zone gives your child priority at that school. Most zoned schools make kindergarten offers to all students living in the zone who apply on time, but not every school can. Applications and offers go through MySchools.

Always confirm your exact address on schoolsearch.schools.nyc before applying.
