770 zone boundaries from the NYC Department of Education. Colored by the zoned school's DOE quality ratings or state test results. Click anywhere to see which school is zoned for that block.

Living in a zone gives your child top priority at that school, not a guaranteed seat. List your zoned school on your MySchools kindergarten application. You can rank it anywhere; your priority is the same. Each school’s “My Chances” section on MySchools shows which priority groups got offers last year.

Zones change year to year. Always confirm an exact address on schoolsearch.schools.nyc before applying.
