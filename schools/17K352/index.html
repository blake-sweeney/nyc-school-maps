The zone

The zone covers parts of Crown Heights (North), Prospect Heights, Prospect Lefferts Gardens-Wingate and Crown Heights (South).

Streets in the zone include Parkside Avenue, Eastern Parkway, Coney Island Avenue, Flatbush Avenue, Washington Avenue, Park Cir, Ocean Avenue and Crooke Avenue.

Zone lines can change, and a block can sit on a border. Always confirm your exact address on schoolsearch.schools.nyc before applying.
