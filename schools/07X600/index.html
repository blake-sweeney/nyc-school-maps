Before you start

  • This is an independent map made by a NYC parent. It isn’t made by, affiliated with or endorsed by NYC Public Schools.
  • Zone lines come from the DOE’s Find a School map (Oct 2026). Ratings, test scores and programs come from the DOE’s 2024–25 School Quality Snapshot. Data can be out of date or have errors.
  • Zones change from year to year, and blocks near a boundary are easy to misread on a map.
  • Living in a zone gives your child priority at that school. Most zoned schools make kindergarten offers to all students living in the zone who apply on time, but not every school can. Applications and offers go through MySchools.

Terms & privacy

Use at your own risk. This map is provided as is, for general information only. It isn’t official enrollment, legal or educational advice, and it may contain errors. Don’t rely on it alone for any decision.

Data. School data belongs to its sources (NYC Public Schools and the City of New York) and is used under their open-data terms. The map’s code is open source under the AGPL-3.0.

Privacy. There are no accounts, no cookies and no ads. Nothing you type is stored by this site. A few display settings, like whether you’ve seen this note, are kept only in your own browser.

  • Addresses you search are sent to NYC Planning’s GeoSearch to find the location.
  • Visits are counted with GoatCounter, which doesn’t use cookies or collect personal data.
  • Map tiles load from the City of New York, the map library from Cloudflare’s cdnjs and fonts from Google Fonts. Like any website, those servers see your IP address.
