The zone

This zone is shared with One World Middle School at Edenwald, Baychester Middle School. Families who live here get zoned priority at each of these schools.

Streets in the zone include Boston Road, New England Thruway, Conner Street, Provost Avenue, East 233rd Street, 229th Drive North, Hollers Avenue and Pratt Avenue.

Zone lines can change, and a block can sit on a border. Always confirm your exact address on schoolsearch.schools.nyc before applying.
