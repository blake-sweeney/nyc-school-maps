The zone

This zone is shared with J.H.S. 123 James M. Kieran. Families who live here get zoned priority at each of these schools.

Streets in the zone include Bronx River Parkway, Bruckner Boulevard, Sheridan Boulevard, White Plains Road, Close Avenue, Morrison Avenue, Westchester Avenue and Edgewater Road.

Zone lines can change, and a block can sit on a border. Always confirm your exact address on schoolsearch.schools.nyc before applying.
