The zone

This zone is shared with The Ivan Lafayette Early Childhood School of the A. Families who live here get zoned priority at each of these schools.

Streets in the zone include 94th Street, Junction Boulevard, 32nd Avenue, 37th Avenue, 95th Street, 97th Street and Jackson Mill Road.

Zone lines can change, and a block can sit on a border. Always confirm your exact address on schoolsearch.schools.nyc before applying.
