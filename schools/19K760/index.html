The zone

This zone is shared with I.S. 171 Abraham Lincoln. Families who live here get zoned priority at each of these schools.

Streets in the zone include Pine Street, Adler Place, Campus Place, Cox Place, Cypress Court, Danforth Street, Eldert Lane and Etna Street.

Zone lines can change, and a block can sit on a border. Always confirm your exact address on schoolsearch.schools.nyc before applying.
