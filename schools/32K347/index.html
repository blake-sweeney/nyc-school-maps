The zone

This zone is shared with I.S. 349 Math, Science & Tech.. Families who live here get zoned priority at each of these schools.

Streets in the zone include Renaissance Court, Suydam Street, Hart Street, Willoughby Avenue, Melrose Street, Jefferson Street, Myrtle Avenue and Dekalb Avenue.

Zone lines can change, and a block can sit on a border. Always confirm your exact address on schoolsearch.schools.nyc before applying.
