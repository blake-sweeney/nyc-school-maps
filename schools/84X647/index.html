How to get in

Charter schools are free public schools run by independent nonprofits. Admission is by lottery only: charter schools can’t screen, so there are no tests, grades, interviews or auditions. Lottery priority usually goes to siblings, then to families who live in District 8.

Apply through the common charter application or on the school’s own site, by April 1 for most schools. It’s separate from MySchools: applying doesn’t affect your MySchools application.

Apply

Charter schools run their own lotteries, outside MySchools. Most take applications through the common charter application, usually by April 1; some use their own form. Families can apply to charter schools and through MySchools in the same year.

Before you start

  • This is an independent map made by a NYC parent. It isn’t made by, affiliated with or endorsed by NYC Public Schools.
  • Zone lines come from the DOE’s Find a School map (Oct 2026). Ratings, test scores and programs come from the DOE’s 2024–25 School Quality Snapshot. Data can be out of date or have errors.
  • Zones change from year to year, and blocks near a boundary are easy to misread on a map.
  • Living in a zone gives your child priority at that school. Most zoned schools make kindergarten offers to all students living in the zone who apply on time, but not every school can. Applications and offers go through MySchools.

Always confirm your exact address on schoolsearch.schools.nyc before applying.
