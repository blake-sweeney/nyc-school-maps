The zone

The zone covers parts of Flatlands, East Flatbush-Farragut, East Flatbush-Erasmus and East Flatbush-Rugby.

Streets in the zone include Kings Highway, Foster Avenue, Connector, Ave D, Ames Lane, Rockaway Parkway, Ralph Avenue and Church Avenue.

Zone lines can change, and a block can sit on a border. Always confirm your exact address on schoolsearch.schools.nyc before applying.
