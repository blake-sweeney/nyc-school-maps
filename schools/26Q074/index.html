The zone

The zone covers parts of Oakland Gardens-Hollis Hills, Bayside, Bellerose and Auburndale.

Streets in the zone include Horace Harding Expressway, Grand Central Parkway, Clearview Expressway, Union Turnpike, Long Island Expressway, Cunningham Park Sr, Aly Pond Park Sr and Cross Island Parkway.

Zone lines can change, and a block can sit on a border. Always confirm your exact address on schoolsearch.schools.nyc before applying.

Before you start
