The zone

The zone covers parts of Bedford Park, University Heights (North)-Fordham and Norwood.

Streets in the zone include East Kingsbridge Road, East Fordham Road, Valentine Avenue, Grand Concourse, Connector, Botanical Square South, Briggs Avenue and East 197th Street.

Zone lines can change, and a block can sit on a border. Always confirm your exact address on schoolsearch.schools.nyc before applying.
