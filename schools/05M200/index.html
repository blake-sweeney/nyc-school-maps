The zone

The zone covers parts of Harlem (North) and Hamilton Heights-Sugar Hill.

Streets in the zone include Harlem River Drive, Adam Clayton Powell Jr Boulevard, West 145th Street, St Nicholas Avenue, West 148th Street, Bradhurst Avenue, Esplanade Gardens Plaza and Frederick Douglass Boulevard.

Zone lines can change, and a block can sit on a border. Always confirm your exact address on schoolsearch.schools.nyc before applying.
