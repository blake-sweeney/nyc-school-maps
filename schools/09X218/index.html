The zone

This zone is shared with P.S. X114 - Luis Llorens Torres Schools. Families who live here get zoned priority at each of these schools.

The zone covers part of Concourse-Concourse Village.

Streets in the zone include Grand Concourse, Jerome Avenue, East 167th Street, East 161st Street, Connector, Cromwell Avenue, Edward L Grant Highway and Walton Avenue.

Zone lines can change, and a block can sit on a border. Always confirm your exact address on schoolsearch.schools.nyc before applying.
