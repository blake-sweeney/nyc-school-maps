The zone

This zone is shared with The Emerson School. Families who live here get zoned priority at each of these schools.

Streets in the zone include Van Wyck Expressway, Liberty Avenue, 109th Avenue, Sutphin Boulevard, Merrick Boulevard, 107th Avenue, 159th Street and Tuskegee Airmen Way.

Zone lines can change, and a block can sit on a border. Always confirm your exact address on schoolsearch.schools.nyc before applying.
