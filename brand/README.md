## Short bio

For the Facebook page's Intro (101 characters at most) and anywhere else a one-liner fits (92 characters):

```
Free map of every NYC public school zone, with DOE ratings, test scores and charter schools.
```

## YouTube channel description

For YouTube's channel description (1,000 characters at most; this is 965):

```
Finding the right public school in New York City shouldn't take a spreadsheet and a free weekend.

NYC School Zones (nycschoolzones.com) is a free map of every public school zone in the five boroughs. Type in your address to see your zoned elementary, middle and high schools, then compare them with the other choices in your district and across the city, including charter schools.

Each school has a card with its DOE ratings (like "3.7 / 4"), state test scores, and admissions numbers where the city publishes them. There are also pages for every school, every district and every neighborhood.

The videos here walk through how to use it: finding your zone, reading a school card, and comparing your options before applications are due.

Worth noting: the site is independent and not run by the DOE. The data comes straight from the city's own sources, so always double-check with the school and MySchools before you apply.

Questions or ideas? Leave a comment!
```
